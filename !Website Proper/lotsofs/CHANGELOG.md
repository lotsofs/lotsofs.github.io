# Changelog

What changed on lotsofs.com for the people who use it, one section per update of the site, newest first. It starts after the update of 2026-10-19; nothing before that was recorded.

## Unreleased

### Added

- In the settings menu (the cog) you can choose how long the rating pop-ups on the song list stay: from 10 seconds to an hour, until you close them, or not at all. It starts at 3 minutes, as before. The pop-up saying your own rating hasn't been saved yet still shows when they are off.

### Changed

- A score has to be a number from 0 to 10. The score box on the song list only takes what can become one: letters, a minus sign or a number over 10 simply don't go in, and on a phone it brings up a number keypad.
- The pop-ups on the song list that say someone rated a song now show that person's name in their own site colour.
