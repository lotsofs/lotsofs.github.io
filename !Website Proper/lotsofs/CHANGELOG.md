# Changelog

What changed on lotsofs.com for the people who use it, one section per update of the site, newest first. It starts after the update of 2026-10-19; nothing before that was recorded. The music pages keep their own list, on their What's New page at lotsofs.com/music/changes, so they are not in here.

## Unreleased

### Added

- The MTA:SA map list has a Warnings column, with a yellow ! where the site's guess about a map differs from what is set.

### Changed

- On an MTA:SA map's route drawing, nitro pickups are now red, repair pickups lime and vehicle change pickups cyan.
- An MTA:SA map's Vehicles via changes lists every vehicle, not just the first ten.

## 2026-11-18

### Added

- Bass tab sheets at lotsofs.com/basstabs: shorthand sheets for 5-string bass, with note names, fretboards for the chords, and A4 printing. Nine songs so far, from The Cardigans to The Smashing Pumpkins.
- A catalogue of old MTA:SA race maps at lotsofs.com/mtasa, linked from the home page: search and filter about 6,800 maps, open each one for its details and a drawing of its route, and comment on them with an account.
- A games section at lotsofs.com/games, for invited accounts: an Age of Empires scenario timeline to tick off as you play, and Sims 2 checklists.
